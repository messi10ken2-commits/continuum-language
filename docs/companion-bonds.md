# Companion bonds and profile avatars

Eight painted companions use independent head/body/tail parts, with happy eyes and finite reactions. Bird/fox/cat/dog are free choices. Active Premium unlocks capybara, guinea pig, quokka and panda as companions. On expiry, they are locked again and the active companion falls back to Bird; the saved Premium preference and bond records are preserved for renewal. Already-earned profile avatars remain available after Premium ends. Companion entitlement and avatar selection are validated on the server.

Each language and companion retains its chapter credits. Existing completed history is backfilled once for newly introduced companions; existing companion credits are preserved. Newly completed chapters credit the selected companion once, with no repeat farming or bond decay. Thresholds are now 5 chapters for greeting, 15 for sound, 30 for delighted reaction. The separate five-second C1 celebration requires an exercise-source pass at the authored pass mark for EVERY basic Learn C1 lesson, including its final summary. A final summary pass alone no longer unlocks it.

The fifth reward is a companion profile avatar: 40 chapters together in one learning language, 20 distinct learning days in that language, and active Premium when earned. Days derive from dated, server-saved exercise attempts for that language in the account timezone; they need not be consecutive. Legacy day evidence is reused. Avatar unlocks are permanent and usable globally in the account/header after earning; initials remain an option. No account name/photo or billing data is overwritten. Privacy scopes and teacher records are unchanged.

Routes: authenticated learner GET/PUT /api/adventure, plus GET/PUT /api/companion-avatar. Persistent additive tables track Premium character grants, language-specific avatar achievements and selected profile icon. Account deletion cascades through all new records. Audio plays only on explicit tap, stops on navigation/change/reaction, and motion respects reduced-motion preference.

Existing animal recordings keep their original credits/licenses. The four new characters use original synthesized game-character sounds (not wildlife recordings or human speech), explicitly disclosed in recording credits. Prepared WebP assets and AAC audio are hosted locally. Source sheets and SHA256 hashes are in the art manifest; no generation service is required at runtime.

Validation: all-language threshold/C1 model tests, API Premium protection, 40-chapter/20-day avatar earning and post-expiry retention tests, account isolation, historical-credit and duplicate protection, full QA, translated interface audit, production build and browser checks at 320/390/768/1440px in both layouts. Browser flow selects Panda, earns/sets its avatar, verifies the header update and distinct finale sound/motion. No physical iPhone Safari testing was available.
