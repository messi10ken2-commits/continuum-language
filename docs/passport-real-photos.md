# Real photographs in the journey passport

The original 211 destination definitions were recovered from the supplied earlier conversation: 70 English, 46 Spanish, 46 Portuguese and 49 Japanese destinations. The earlier conversation referred to source files under `.media-build/passport-source/photos/` in its former workspace; those original photo files were absent from the surviving directories. The current collection is reacquired from Commons using those destination definitions. It must not be described as recovery of byte-identical copies of all 158 previously downloaded files.

`public/passport-real/catalog.json` contains only acquired, usable real photos with author, source, license and SHA-256. `destinations.json` retains the complete recovered editorial list. Local recovery status records unresolved sources; failed queries do not become fake real-photo entries. Real Commons photographs take priority. Chapters without an acquired Commons photo use the existing eight credited regional photographs before generated scenery. The final reviewed release contains 183 Commons photos; 28 recovered destination entries remain unresolved or excluded. Final assets use content-hash filenames, separate from acquisition working files. Real destinations have stable chapter assignments based on the recovered per-language list, so later additions do not move existing photos to a different achievement.

Photo details display source and licensing attribution. Resized/cropped derivatives retain their source license. Companion images are separate generated creative scenes: all eight existing companion sets are supported, selecting the matching city by learning language. The passport includes the selected companion's keepsake after an earned full level, return or three-day milestone; the C1 animation retains its existing requirements; companion selection and membership gates are unchanged.

Acquisition and source review exclude maps, drawings, scanned book pages and unsuitable close-ups. Country/place names are editorial destination labels, not assertions about legal language status or travel recommendations. No learning records or deployment secrets enter the photo files or credits.
