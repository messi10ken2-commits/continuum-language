# C1 everyday idioms and proverbs

Each of English, Japanese, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese gains one ordinary C1 chapter with two learning lessons and a checkpoint. The learning lessons teach five expressions each, with meaning, register, sentence pattern, original model, translation and an entry-specific watch-out. English includes the ten requested expressions; Japanese mixes 四字熟語, 慣用句 and ことわざ. Spanish and Portuguese teach their own locutions and sayings rather than literal translations of the English list.

The ordinary player offers the same reference, contextual interpretation, recall, listening and word-tile construction flow as the existing course. Expanded practice covers every taught expression. Checkpoints draw four fresh situations from a ten-item bank, with both lessons represented. New C1 summary sessions include this chapter. Their explicit seed prefix keeps older saved C1 summary seeds on their earlier question pool. No existing lesson, chapter or exclusive-course identifiers are renumbered.

Two additional exclusive C1 workshops per language teach four further expressions each, with eight mixed tasks: interpretation, listening, dictation, cloze, ordering and register-sensitive selection. Examples differ from the ordinary lessons. Existing Premium access checks, hidden answer keys, shuffled sessions, exact-text audio endpoints and completion storage remain in force. Speech uses the existing studio/device pipeline and locale settings; no new recorded-native-speaker claim is made.

Both visual maps derive the new chapter and its three activities from the shared journey model, before the final level summary. Compact labels keep map nodes readable. Three-node panorama panels use vertically separated positions with edge clearance. Four new illustrated passport stamps reuse the existing original watercolor compositions; `build-passport-art.cjs --missing-only` preserves all previously registered artwork and hashes.

Headings have reviewed translations in all nine non-English display languages. New exclusive-course instructions and feedback are also included in the offline Korean, Indonesian, Russian and Arabic catalogs; the ordinary lesson text enters the existing protected translation corpus.
